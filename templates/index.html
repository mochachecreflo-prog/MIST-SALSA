Dance Styles We Teach

Salsa (LA & Cuban)

Fast-paced, vibrant, and energetic. Master core footwork, cross-body leads, and social partner work.

Sensual Bachata

Smooth rhythms from the Dominican Republic. Focus on body movement, connection, and musicality.

Kompa

Haitian social dance emphasizing smooth hip movements, relaxed rhythms, and lead-follow harmony.
