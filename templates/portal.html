Dance Styles We Teach

🔥 Salsa

Fast-paced, energetic, and full of fluid footwork and turn patterns. Suitable for all skill levels!

❤️ Bachata

Sensual and rhythmic dance originating from the Dominican Republic, perfect for learning rhythm and connection.

🎶 Kompa

Smooth Haitian dance genre emphasizing subtle hip movements, flow, and musical expression.
